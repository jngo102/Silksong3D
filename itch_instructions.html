Windows
If you launch the downloaded executable file and a warning popup shows up saying that it protected your PC, click More Info and then Run Anyway.

macOS
Double click the downloaded zip file, then double click the app that appears beside it to launch the game. If a warning popup appears saying that it could not verify the game, dismiss it by clicking Done, then open the System Settings app, go to Privacy & Security in the left sidebar, scroll down to Security, and click Open Anyway next to the game name.

Linux
You may need to give yourself access permission to launch the downloaded binary, i.e. chmod +x silksong3d-linux.x86_64
